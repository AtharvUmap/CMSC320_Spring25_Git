Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: The key difference between merging and rebasing is whether or not the history is preserved. Merging preserves the history both of the main branch and the child branch, however rebasing simply appends the changes into a new commit and removes the development commits/history of rebased branch.
